# Efficiency evaluator methods

Twelve experimental additive corrections preserve practical-combined as the baseline. Every result is bounded to [-7,7] points. Gameover and zero-future-action states return twelve zeros. No playing-strength evidence is implied; the coordinator owns integration and tournament launches.

Only own hand/bonus objectives and public tableaux, food inventories, feeder and action counts are used. Predator card odds use a fixed 170-card catalog prior; no deck, random state, opponent hand/bonuses or tucked identities are inspected. Imports are catalog/scoring only, avoiding the leaf/core cycle.

Common quantities: T = own current remaining actions plus later rounds; A = min(5,T/3). Open egg room is capped by current global supply. Typed hand demand excludes birds with no compatible open habitat; either-food demand is divided across acceptable types. A food token's shadow price is .18 plus up to .55 for current typed deficit and up to .12 for future opportunities (maximum .85). Cards are .15 to .60 depending on hand scarcity. Food affordability respects specified types, wild costs, either choices and 2:1 substitution. Play projections remove the hand card and pay canonical column eggs before exact bonus scoring; food opportunity uses the minimum number of actual tokens required, including substitution.

| Name | Formula/mechanism |
| --- | --- |
| engine-net-income | Sum currently realizable brown power yields, subtract .45 per brown bird, multiply by .5A. Egg/card and egg/food exchanges subtract their egg payment; paid double tucks subtract a food shadow price. Hand tucks subtract retained-card opportunity. |
| engine-saturation | For supply food, penalize .35 per produced token beyond unmet typed hand demand plus .35 speculative future demand; penalize full egg capacity .6 per egg power; card powers incur .035 per card beyond T. Multiply by A. |
| shared-power-net | Subtract largest public opponent benefit for shared food/eggs, one expected card-value for shared draw, .4 for shared feeder picks; multiply by .65A. Shared eggs require matching free nests and global supply. |
| pink-event-model | Up to min(4,.5T) once-between-turn intervals minus current used flag. Public opponent grassland size gives egg-event chance; open forest/wetland space gives bird-play chance; public predators and fixed catalog odds give predator-event chance; rodent feeder presence changes rodent-event likelihood. Combine opponents as 1-product(1-p), so income cannot exceed one trigger per interval. Egg forecasts consume available matching room and current global supply. |
| predator-expectation | A times success probability minus .5 per brown predator. Card predicates are exact catalog proportions with strict wingspan <75/<100/<50; dice success is 1-(5/6)^(5-feeder size). Thus full feeder correctly disables outside-dice hunting. |
| repeat-resource-cost | A times best eligible same-row target's net yield minus .65 per repeat power. Excludes other repeaters and restricts predator repeaters. Paid tuck repetition requires two tokens when its original target still activates later, one when original already activated; right-to-left prior food producers add to availability. |
| tuck-card-opportunity | Discount retained hand birds by currently payable net points plus exact bonus increment minus column eggs, .65 per actual food token and 1.5 action opportunity. Penalize consuming the cheapest retained card beyond tuck reward when no earlier same-row card source exists. Tuck-and-draw still loses a valuable initial card; unknown replacement is not assumed equivalent. |
| feeder-access | For matching feeder powers, actual present matching dice (or .5 refill estimate) determine available benefit. Subtract .6 per cache-seed power or .45 per other feeder power; multiply by min(3,A). Dual faces count only as seed/invertebrate. |
| late-conversion | T<=5 only: .6 times best currently payable bird net points or one egg-row action, minus base egg harvest; subtract .035 per stored food times (6-T) to discourage stranded inventory. Each candidate pays actual food and column eggs; exact bonus change is included. |
| phase-switch | T>10 emphasizes the stronger forest/wetland net production rate over min(3,A) actions; 5<T<=10 uses strongest current habitat production; T<=5 switches to concrete late conversion. |
| robust-value | Compare the same alternatives (one egg action or one payable bird) under two explicit food scenarios: scarcity prices spent tokens at .85 and retained usable stock at .55; surplus prices spent tokens at .25 and stock at .10. Optimize the action separately in each scenario, take the lower value, subtract base egg harvest and multiply by .45. This tests realization sensitivity rather than taking a random minimum of unrelated evaluations. |
| scarcity-portfolio | Complementary penalties/recovery: .35 saturation + .35 shared-power net + .30 tuck opportunity + .35 robust value, then final cap. |

Limitations: future activation allocation, event probabilities, unknown card quality and food shadow prices are heuristics. Shared benefits are discounted by the strongest individual opponent, not an exact multiplayer utility. State food inventories are public but future opponent hand demand is deliberately unknown. Repeated costs are checked conservatively; full activation sequences, opponent responses, future feeder rerolls and bonus-preserving alternative egg payments are not searched. Global egg supply and nest capacity cap present forecasts but future depletion/replenishment is not simulated. Some rare power codes have zero yield estimate. No candidate pruning or legal-action generator changes are included.

Validation command: `node --test test/wingspan-efficiency-evaluators.test.js` (16 tests). Semantic fixtures cover all twelve families, explicit repeat double-payment, a lone valuable tuck-and-draw card, pink finite capacity, exact catalog prior and dice odds, purity, deterministic bounded outputs, terminal/actionless zeros and hidden-data access traps. `node scripts/check-docs.mjs` is the documentation gate. Neither command measures playing strength.
