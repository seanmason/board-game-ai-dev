# Opening-plan experiment

This separate experiment broadens setup candidates and adds a small round-one correction to practical-combined. It changes no production default by itself. The integrating caller owns its separate `opening-plan` leaf name and invocation of the setup generator. Earlier hand-only planning performed poorly, so this method preserves all old setup alternatives, adds diversity, caps the point correction at 3, and expires after round one. No mandatory forest bonus is used.

## Setup alternatives

For the original five known hand cards, enumerate every card subset and every food-discard subset of equal size: sum(k=0..5) C(5,k)^2 = 252 combinations. Evaluate each with every offered own bonus (normally 504 actions). Invalid negative food inventories are rejected. Existing actions are retained verbatim as the prefix; canonical keys prevent duplicate additions. With the normal eight existing setup candidates, return at most 24 actions. A caller supplying more than 24 existing candidates retains them all, rather than silently pruning an existing policy.

Rank using the two-placement option estimate below, plus .12 times exact current retained bonus points, minus .025 per retained bird. Sort ties deterministically by canonical action key. Before filling by score, reserve the best immediately payable option for each possible first bird, then each habitat, then immediate/one-food-away/pair families and each offered bonus. This searches the full setup exchange space but deliberately prunes its returned action list; it is not exhaustive setup search in the rollout.

## Concrete placement estimate

Food payment uses `core.findFoodPayment`, including either-food costs, wild symbols and 2:1 substitution. Inventory per type is capped at six for bounded payment enumeration; no bird needs more than six actual tokens. An option must be payable now or after adding one food of some type. First placements require an open compatible habitat and enough existing eggs, or one egg action backed by current empty nest capacity and current global supply.

Bird quality Q = .23 printed points + .07 egg capacity + production estimate. Production is .65 for brown supply food/cache codes 25/57/66/31, .45 for brown tuck-draw/draw codes 4/34/56, .50 for brown egg codes 14/30, .20 for shared brown food/card codes 16/40/59/60/63, .15 for other brown powers, .25 for pink powers and zero for white/no power. These are deliberately modest opportunity estimates, not exact future power income.

First placement delay D1 = one placement action + zero/one food-gather action + zero/one egg action. Its score is Q1/D1. Debit its actual food payment, then consider each distinct second hand bird and compatible open habitat. The second option must be payable from the remaining inventory or after one additional food. Its column egg requirement is screened against remaining eggs or empty capacity including the first bird and available supply; first-stage egg-action placement and supply consumption are approximated, so this is not a complete proof of pair-sequence legality. D2 counts its placement, food and egg actions similarly. A capacity-screened pair scores Q1/D1 + .45 × (Q2 + .45 × actual second-placement row-output jump)/(D1+D2). Keep the maximum of first-only and pair options. No multiple second cards are summed.

## Leaf correction

Only round one receives a correction. A player still awaiting setup gets zero. Fade = own current remaining actions / 8 (clamped to [0,1]); zero remaining actions and terminal states return zero. Correction = min(3, (min(1,.35 × best placement-option score) + min(2,.7 × sum of the two highest installed bird quality estimates)) × fade). Installed credit is deliberately stronger than pending credit, so playing a useful bird does not erase its opening option value. At most two installed bird qualities contribute. This preserves the baseline, fades linearly within round one, and becomes exactly zero thereafter.

Limitations: this is a two-placement option heuristic, not a forced opening sequence or a full action-budget planner. A one-food-away option assumes the desired food can be gathered later, without guaranteeing the current feeder face. Food actions producing more than one required food are not exploited; white resource/egg powers and detailed brown activation order are not simulated. The first food payment follows the core's canonical minimum payment rather than optimizing every payment for the second bird. Egg actions use capacity feasibility and approximate one-action timing; no exact egg placement sequence or bonus-preserving egg payment is searched. Bonus scoring affects setup ranking only through current retained bonus points. Opponent choices, private cards and hidden decks are not read.

Validation: `node --test test/wingspan-opening-strategy.test.js` passes seven tests: preserved candidates/cap/determinism/purity; every returned setup action applies legally and validates on four seeds; previously omitted payable singleton; bounded fading/expiry; one-food-away versus immediate payment; hidden/private access traps; nonsetup passthrough; legal productive first placement does not lose pending opening credit. `node scripts/check-docs.mjs` passes with the completed report linked. No tournament is launched by this module task; playing strength remains unestablished.
