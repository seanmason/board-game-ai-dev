# Hive — original eleven-piece implementation handoff

Planning only, researched 2026-10-18. Read [COMMON.md](COMMON.md) first; its stable interfaces, owned paths and validation/AI budgets govern implementation. Implement the original Gen42 base game for two players, eleven pieces each, under the classic English rulebook below. Exclude Mosquito, Ladybug, Pillbug, Carbon/Pocket bundled expansion pieces, Ultimate opening changes, clocks and tournament restrictions. The setup must display this edition choice: classic rules allow a Queen opening. Do not advertise all contemporary Hive editions.

## Popularity and primary evidence

The [BGG ratings page](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/2655/hive/ratings) returned 40,863 ratings, average 7.313, overall rank 338, abstract rank 19, weight 2.31/5. This is a retrieved/indexed snapshot, not an API-certified live count. The [Gen42 product page](https://www.gen42.com/product/hive/) independently lists the 22 base pieces and two-player scope. Popularity supports selection; it says nothing about implementation quality or human AI strength.

Authority: [Gen42 classic English rules, copyright 2010](https://www.gen42.com/wp-content/uploads/Hive-rules.pdf). Printed page groups: preparation/object and placing pp1–2; Queen deadline and movement permission p3; Queen p4; Beetle p5; Grasshopper p6; Spider p7; Soldier Ant p8; One Hive p9; freedom to move/pass p10; endgame p11. Preserve a verified page-map/hash when fixtures are created; this website can replace its PDF. No separately authenticated publisher FAQ resolving every elevated gate was located. Community FAQs or digital implementations may identify cases, but must not silently override this edition.

Rule digest: each side has Q1/B2/G3/S2/A3, face-up reserves. Place a tile or move one; first placements touch, later placements touch only friendly visible top colours. Queen enters by own fourth turn; movement requires own Queen already placed. Pieces remain connected, including during transit. Queen slides once; Spider slides exactly three without backtracking; Ant follows any sliding path; Grasshopper jumps contiguous occupied hexes straight to first vacancy; Beetle steps/climbs, immobilizes covered tiles and controls stack colour. Surround six Queen neighbours to win; simultaneous surrounds draw. Forced pass only if no placement/move. Cyclic stalemate can be agreed drawn. Source groups above govern the digest.

## Engine design and complete action contract

Own `hive/`: `catalog.js`, `core.js`, `geometry.js`, `actions.js`, `ai.js`, `worker.js`, `observations.js`, `app.js`, `styles.css`, `index.html`; own tests under `test/hive*.test.js` and game-specific scripts/overview as assigned in COMMON. These are proposed paths, not existing deliverables. The coordinator owns all root menus, shared scheduling/session files, asset allowlists, packaging and documentation indexes. Follow the existing shared worker/session contracts without changing them from a game subtask.

Use sparse axial coordinates `(q,r)` with six fixed direction vectors and arrays of piece IDs bottom-to-top. Stable IDs include colour/type/ordinal; never replace the covered piece with the Beetle in the state. Store per-player turns taken, starting seat, current player, reserve counts, phase/result, public move history and adjudication events. State validation accounts for exactly 22 pieces, legal stacks, one Queen per colour, one connected occupied-cell graph and matching reserves. Do not make reachability claims from a valid snapshot alone.

Pure `legalActions(state)` must enumerate all human placements and legal destinations. `applyAction` validates against the same complete rule predicates and returns a fresh state. Search may order or explicitly cap moves; the move editor cannot use a pruned AI list. Actions: `{type:'place',pieceId,to}`, `{type:'move',pieceId,from,to,path?}`, and forced `{type:'pass'}`. Administrative resign/agreed-draw/correction are separate recorded events. Multiple Spider/Ant paths to the same destination can share one canonical action while retaining a witness path for explanation. Different same-type reserve ordinals are physically equivalent; select the lowest available ordinal and allow identity reconciliation during physical import.

Required move-generation algorithm:

1. Placement frontier is all empty neighbours of occupied cells. First move can normalize to origin without restricting physical coordinates; second seat's first placement can use all six adjacent cells. Subsequent placement colour tests inspect stack tops. Fourth-turn Queen obligation must be enforced before allowing other placements; if no action exists, follow forced pass rather than inventing automatic defeat. Count own turns, not total plies. Movement never reuses placement colour restrictions.
2. Only owned top tiles can move, after Queen permission. Pop the mover and test occupied-cell connectivity before looking for its destination; reconnecting the hive at the end cannot repair an illegal temporary split. Removing an upper Beetle usually leaves its source cell occupied and therefore must not receive the same articulation restriction as removing its bottom tile.
3. For ground slides, test each edge after lifting the mover: destination empty and exactly one of the two common flanking neighbours occupied. Two occupied flanks close the gate; zero loses continuous contact while crossing, even when source and destination each separately touch other hive cells. Ant BFS explores reachable perimeter cells without arbitrary coordinate radius. Spider DFS has depth exactly three, no revisits/backtracking, and uninterrupted contact; destinations from two-step routes alone are invalid. Neither may return to its start as a pass substitute.
4. Grasshopper examines each of six rays: adjacent cell must be occupied, continue over contiguous occupied coordinates regardless of stack height, land at first empty coordinate. No skipping a gap or choosing an earlier occupied landing. Connectivity is still checked at lift.
5. Beetles need height-aware edges, not a blanket exemption from gates. The geometric predicate uses stationary source/destination support heights after lifting; an edge is blocked when both flanking stacks exceed `max(sourceSupport,destinationSupport)`. Ground-to-ground also requires continuous hive contact as in step3. Validate the geometry against the primary Beetle/freedom-to-move diagrams and independent physical fixtures before claiming elevated-gate certification; this is an implementation acceptance gate, not an optional omitted movement rule. Cover climbing onto friendly/enemy stacks, moving across stacks, descending into holes, and being blocked by taller stacked Beetles. Initial placement is always on empty ground.
6. Evaluate both Queens' six-neighbour occupation after the action, finding Queens even under Beetles. A covered Queen is not itself captured. Check simultaneous surround before single-winner assignment. No score, capture count or material tiebreak exists.

## Repetition, physical recording and observations

No hidden cards or random draws exist in this scope; reserve identities, stack order and all positions are public. The app must still distinguish recorded physical facts from a search continuation. A claimed stack colour cannot stand in for a missing underlying identity. Unknown physical stack order should block advice until reconciled, not be sampled as if secret gameplay.

Normalize translation for hashes, include side-to-move/reserves/Queen-deadline status, and keep enough history to identify cycles. Search can score an ancestor cycle as a draw-like estimate to terminate analysis, clearly distinct from a recorded game result. Classic rules support agreed cyclic stalemate; do not auto-declare a chess-style threefold or 32-ply draw. Offer a reviewable draw agreement event. Two consecutive forced passes should trigger a stalemate review and preserve history; do not silently substitute a platform-specific rule for the pinned book.

Physical flow: choose reserve tile or top tile, then highlighted destination; show source/destination plus path/climb text before explicit confirmation. Confirming records exactly one complete turn. Undo/redo restores board, reserves, deadline counters, result and search-generation ID atomically. Corrections may relocate/reidentify a tile, reorder a stack or fix the side to act; preview the entire corrected snapshot and validate before replacing the live record. Orientation/translation adjustment is view metadata and must not change move semantics. Retain optional pre-correction snapshot and reason. Import/export carries edition and rule profile; reject expansion pieces with a clear scope message.

## AI and useful low-budget evidence

Perfect-information adversarial search fits this game better than hidden-world rollouts. Begin with iterative-deepening negamax/alpha-beta, transposition table, deterministic ordering and a cancellable worker. Terminal surround scores dominate all heuristics. Order immediate wins, moves preventing immediate loss, queen escape/mobility, Beetle pins and reserve deployment; exact connectivity/gate checks remain authoritative. Evaluate Queen liberty count, distinct immediate surrounding threats, own/opponent movable pieces and reserve accessibility with bounded weights. Do not reward material as if pieces were captured. Keep opening symmetry reduction internal to search; preserve the full UI action set.

Expose completed depth, nodes, elapsed time, move count and whether selective search was used. A score or tactical win distance is not a calibrated win probability. Proposed first budget: 150–300ms responsive recommendation, extend while worker is running; test a 100ms and 500ms budget rather than assuming deeper is stronger. Retain a completed-depth result if interrupted. Analysis hover/variation paths cannot mutate the physical game.

## Semantic fixtures and acceptance gates

Create independent small fixtures, with axial coordinates and expected destination sets reviewed from real tile arrangements. Minimum cases:

- Every first-placement type, six second-seat placements, Queen permitted first under classic profile, own fourth-turn forced Queen, and no movement before own Queen. A search symmetry shortcut must not remove legal human placements.
- Placement adjacent to own/enemy/mixed top colours, enemy Beetle covering own tile, friendly Beetle covering enemy tile, and no direct reserve placement on stacks.
- Articulation tile pinned despite a reconnecting destination; movable top Beetle above that articulation; covered Queen still present; both colours' tiles can provide winning surrounds.
- Queen closed/open flank gates and zero-common-flank loss of contact; an interior cavity reachable through a legal passage versus a one-edge squeeze; Ant routing around a long hive without a fixed radius; all legal Spider three-step witnesses and rejection of two/four steps, revisit and loss of contact.
- Grasshopper one-tile and multi-tile jumps, six directions, first-empty landing, stack-height independence, invalid adjacent vacancy and interrupted ray.
- Beetle ground slide, climb, stack-top colour, four-Beetle stack above a base tile, trapped top tile under an opponent, descent into surrounded hole, two tall flank blockers and one clear flank. Elevated cases remain primary-clarification gate until evidenced.
- One Queen surrounded, own Queen accidentally surrounded, both Queens surrounded simultaneously, forced pass with no actions, pass rejected with one legal action, cycle detection without automatic game adjudication, agreed draw and undo from terminal.

Property checks: every generated action applies; engine remains immutable; piece/reserve conservation; coordinate translation/rotation/reflection preserve legal destination sets; a slow independent connectivity/slide oracle agrees on bounded random reachable positions; invalid actions preserve the prior record. Test minimum viewport, portrait/landscape, reload recovery, stale worker results after correction and exported/imported history. Original simple graphics suffice; no publisher art redistribution is required.
