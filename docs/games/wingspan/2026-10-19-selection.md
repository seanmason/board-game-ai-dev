## Evidence and decision

The adopted opening package previously won117–83 in fresh200-game confirmation.
The completed evaluator/compute study added2,800 valid games. Its blend won57.5%
in screening, then53.25% in fresh equal-work confirmation (paired95% interval
46.35–60.15%). At matched requested time it won only37.25% against the actual
deployed package (31.04–43.46%), with mean margin−3.785. It failed promotion.

The corrected research reference at300samples beat its100-sample counterpart
with58.25% winner share, paired95% interval51.86–64.64%, and multiplicity-corrected
one-sided lower bound50.411%. This supports sample scaling within that research
pipeline. It does not compare corrected reference300 against the deployed
pipeline, nor establish a new browser budget or superiority from deeper rollout.

The follow-up admitted the full60-pair screen and completed480 strength games
plus8 validation canaries, all valid. No candidate reached its55%-plus-positive-
margin eligibility rule, so the frozen plan scheduled no confirmation:

| Intervention | Winner share versus corrected reference | Mean score margin |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Power-aware continuations | 45.83% | −0.97 |
| Remove persistent action prior | 42.50% | −2.41 |
| Both search changes | 44.58% | −1.48 |
| Lean exceptional/synergy evaluator | 46.67% | −1.44 |

All four passed latency checks. These screen estimates do not prove every
alternative is weaker in every setting. They supply no basis for promotion.
The diagnostic stage reached its predeclared ten-minute limit and retained
partial checkpoints; it is mechanism evidence, not a complete strength study.
