# Base-game Wingspan data provenance

Only the original 170 bird species, 26 bonus goals and eight paired goal
tiles are present. The 10 Swift-Start promotional birds are intentionally
excluded. No regional expansion, bird illustration, publisher layout,
flavor text or literal power description is included.

Bird names are real species names; numeric card features and mechanical
attributes are curated from the [MIT-licensed Wingspan R data project]
(https://github.com/coolbutuseless/wingspan) and cross-checked against the
community spreadsheet used by Morgan-Griffiths. The upstream community data
ultimately derives from the 2019 Wingspan cards. All art, card prose and
rulebook content remain copyrighted by their respective creators/publisher.
The source project's MIT license does not grant us the publisher's artwork
rights. Attribution does not imply endorsement or affiliation.

Each bird has a numeric \`powerCode\` referring to a stable power-effect
archetype; no copyrighted power text is bundled. Some archetypes involve
many distinct user choices, conditional player effects or card swaps. An
effect is not considered implemented until its handler and test exist.
The original rules and their appendix are the authority in any discrepancy.

\`birds.json\` and \`bonuses.json\` are data, not scripts. Do not reproduce
images or proprietary rulebook passages in code or UI.
